# Financial Performance and Fundamental Analysis of BRAC Bank PLC (FY2021-FY2025)

An independent, equity-research style analysis of BRAC Bank PLC, benchmarked against The City Bank and Eastern Bank PLC. Built from 15 published annual reports (five per bank). Educational research, not investment advice.

## Files
| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `01_Research_Report.pdf` | 29-page report: company overview, financial statement analysis, ratios, trends, peer comparison, risk assessment |
| `02_Presentation.pdf` | 10-slide summary |
| `03_Financial_Model.xlsx` | Formula-driven Excel model (10 sheets). All inputs are on the **Raw Data** sheet with source and page; every other sheet is formulas |
| `04_Carousel_Summary.pdf` | 9-page visual summary |

## Headline findings
- Consolidated net profit rose about 4.8x (BDT 4.7 bn to 22.5 bn) while net interest income fell 22% from its FY2023 peak. Investment income is now 56% of operating income.
- 19% of FY2025 consolidated profit belongs to minority holders (mainly bKash); profit attributable to BRAC Bank shareholders is BDT 18.2 bn.
- NPL ratio improved from 3.90% to 2.27%, but classified loans were flat in taka terms.
- Net margins are almost identical across the three banks (about 13%); differences in ROE come from asset yield and leverage.

## Method
- Primary sources only: the banks' annual reports for 2021 to 2025. Restated figures use the latest published value and are logged.
- Consolidated and standalone figures are never mixed in one ratio; the peer comparison is standalone for all three banks.
- Calculated ratios (NPL, cost-to-income, provision coverage, capital ratios, ROA) reproduce each bank's reported figures. Balance sheet, profit and cash-flow checks are within BDT 1 million.

## Limitations
Two peers only; standalone comparison only; the banks' reported net interest margins use different definitions and are not compared; causes of movements are the author's interpretation.

## Data sources
BRAC Bank PLC, The City Bank, and Eastern Bank annual reports 2021 to 2025 (publicly available from each bank's website; not redistributed here).
